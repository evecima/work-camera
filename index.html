Your pictures stay in Work Camera's private local storage, not in Apple Photos or a cloud. iOS may clear web-app data; export important images frequently.

Checking photos needing backup…

Exports original files into folders by building, area and apartment. For iPhone reliability, large libraries are divided into batches.

✅ 2 · Verify on your computer

Transfer the ZIP to your computer, open it and check that the photos are present. Preparing or sharing a ZIP does not prove the backup succeeded.

🧹 3 · Free iPhone space

Only photos from a batch you explicitly confirmed can be removed. Deletion is permanent inside Work Camera.

🔐 Privacy & safety

No photos are uploaded from Work Camera. Use only approved workplace images and follow your employer's photo policies. Keep a second copy on another drive when possible.
